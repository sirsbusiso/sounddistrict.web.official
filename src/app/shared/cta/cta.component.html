Submit Your DJ Mix

We welcome submissions from talented DJs creating Deep House, Soulful House, Afro House and underground electronic mixes. Please upload your mix to a cloud storage platform (Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, etc.) and send the link to our email or any of our social media platforms, along with your DJ biography, mix information, social media profiles and booking details. Every submission is carefully reviewed, and we'll get in touch if your mix is selected to be featured on the Sound District Podcast.
